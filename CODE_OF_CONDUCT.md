# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information.

**Additionally**, because this is a security tool: do not use the project's
spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) to solicit, share, or coordinate unauthorized
attacks against systems you do not own. Keep all discussion focused on lawful,
authorized security testing.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers. Maintainers will review and investigate all complaints and
respond in a way they deem appropriate. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
