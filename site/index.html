Catch vulnerabilities
before you deploy.

Most scanners test production — after the bug is already live. WebRecon scans your app on localhost, auto-detects your dev server, and gives a clear go/no-go verdict. A broken build never reaches production.

# start your app, then one command: webrecon predeploy --fail-on high
  • Auto-detects your running dev server (ports 3000, 5173, 8080, 8000…) — no URL needed.
  • Gate on severity — exit code 1 blocks the deploy, 0 ships it. Drops into any deploy script or CI.
  • Git pre-push hook — --install-hook scans automatically before every push.

Beyond scanning

It doesn't just find. It fixes.

Most scanners hand you a list of problems. WebRecon hands you the fix, watches while you code, and grades your security.

Auto-remediationwebrecon fix

Generates the exact config for your stack — nginx, Apache, Express, Flask or Django — for missing headers, cookies, CORS, CSP and more. Paste it in, or --apply it.

webrecon fix http://localhost:3000 --stack nginx

Live security linterwebrecon watch

A test-watcher for vulnerabilities. Re-scans on an interval or the moment you save a file, and shows only what's new or fixed — feedback while you code.

webrecon watch --watch-dir ./src

Security-grade badgewebrecon badge

An A–F security grade as a shareable SVG for your README — generated offline from the scan. Put it next to your build badge.

Capabilities

A full workflow, not just a scanner

From reconnaissance to reporting to continuous monitoring — everything you need to find, prioritise, and track vulnerabilities.

26+ OWASP-aligned checks

Injection, XSS, SSTI, SSRF, XXE, IDOR, CSRF, CORS, JWT, GraphQL, secrets-in-JS and more — each finding ships with a copy-paste proof of concept.

sqlixssssrfsstixxeidorjwtgraphql

Exploit intelligence

Detected versions are matched to CVEs and ranked by EPSS and CISA KEV — and flagged when a public Exploit-DB or Metasploit weapon exists. Fix what's actually being exploited first.

Blind-vuln confirmation

A built-in OAST listener confirms blind SSRF and RCE out-of-band — turning "maybe" into "confirmed".

History & diff

Every scan is saved locally. See exactly what's new or fixed since your last run.

Continuous monitoring

Watch a target on a schedule and get alerted via Slack, Discord, webhook or email the moment a new issue appears.

Rate-limit advisor

Detects missing rate limiting and recommends the right algorithm per endpoint — sliding window, token bucket, or leaky bucket.

Extensible by design

Add detections with drop-in YAML templates — Nuclei-style, no code. Bring your own CVE database and wordlists.
